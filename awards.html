2026 · 661 Squadron

Lord Strathcona Medal

I received the Lord Strathcona Medal at 661 Squadron’s Annual Ceremonial Review on June 2, 2026.

The national award recognizes excellence in cadet training, physical fitness, and commitment. For me, it marks an important point in a cadet experience that began in 2021 and grew into instruction and mentorship responsibilities.

It is a milestone I connect to the everyday work of participating, preparing, and setting an example for others.

2026 · Air Cadets

Air Cadet Service Medal

I received the Air Cadet Service Medal at the June 2026 Annual Ceremonial Review. The Air Cadet League’s award recognizes at least four years of continuous meritorious cadet service.

For me, this medal represents sustained participation: returning each training year, accepting new responsibilities, and developing from a junior cadet into a senior leadership role.

2026 · 661 Squadron

Top Cadet Instructor

I received Top Cadet Instructor recognition at 661 Squadron’s 2026 Annual Ceremonial Review.

Instruction is one of the parts of cadets I value most. Explaining a subject requires preparation, but delivering a useful lesson also means noticing whether people understand and adjusting how the material is presented.

This recognition is closely connected to my experience mentoring cadets and supporting their learning.

2026 · 661 Squadron

Best Physical Fitness

I received Best Physical Fitness recognition at the squadron’s 2026 Annual Ceremonial Review.

Fitness has been a consistent part of my cadet experience, including Fitness and Sports Instructor training in 2025 and a return to Blackdown as a staff cadet in 2026. It also connects to my interest in rugby and ongoing physical development.

I see this recognition as a marker of consistent effort and a reason to keep improving.

Mock trial · Two awards

Best Advocate

I have received two Best Advocate awards through mock trial.

Advocacy requires more than speaking confidently. It involves understanding the facts, developing a coherent position, and making an argument that someone else can follow. Mock trial gave me practical experience combining preparation with performance under pressure.

These awards reflect an area I want to continue developing through my interests in law, political science, and public service.

Mock trial · One award

Best Closing

I received one Best Closing award through mock trial.

A closing argument brings the evidence and the central argument together. Preparing one gave me practice identifying what matters most, organizing it clearly, and explaining why it supports a conclusion.

This experience strengthened my interest in persuasive writing and disciplined public speaking.

2026 · Royal Canadian Legion, Branch 48

Poppy Appreciation

I received Royal Canadian Legion Poppy Appreciation recognition associated with Branch 48, presented among my June 2026 recognitions.

I include it here as a community recognition alongside my cadet awards. It connects to the importance I place on remembrance and contributing to local service activities.

2023–2024 · Academic recognition

Honour Roll

I achieved Honour Roll recognition for the 2023–2024 school year.

Academic work forms part of the same commitment to preparation and follow-through that I bring to cadets and employment. This recognition is an academic milestone within that wider development.
