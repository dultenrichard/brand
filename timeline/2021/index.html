2021 · A new direction

Joined Royal Canadian Air Cadets

What it is. The beginning of my cadet experience, initially with 851 Prince Edward RCACS.

What it meant to me. At the start I was learning the system from the bottom: drill, dress, expectations, teamwork, and how a squadron functions. I did not join as a leader. I had to grow into responsibility.

Why it matters. The later awards make more sense when this starting point is visible. The progression matters more than pretending the senior roles appeared overnight.

2021 · A new direction

2nd Place · Intermediate Poem

What it is. A second consecutive second-place result in the Royal Canadian Legion Branch 387 Remembrance Day poem competition, this time in the Intermediate category.

What it meant to me. Repeating a result matters differently than earning it once. It showed me that communication could be developed rather than treated as a one-off strength.

Why it matters. It continued a writing record that later became useful in mock trial, public speaking, research, and professional communication.
