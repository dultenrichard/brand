2026 · Senior responsibility

Staff Cadet Sergeant · Blackdown CTC

What it is. Returned to Blackdown in 2026 as a Staff Cadet Sergeant, including two weeks as a platoon sergeant and four weeks in company float/support.

What it meant to me. Staff changed the perspective. I was no longer there only to learn; other cadets, staff, schedules, equipment, and routine problems depended on people doing their part.

Why it matters. That experience sharpened my view that leadership is situational. To be useful, you have to adapt without losing the standard.

2026 · Senior responsibility

Lord Strathcona Medal

What it is. Received at 661 Squadron’s Annual Ceremonial Review on June 2, 2026. It is one of the major national cadet recognitions and is tied to performance in cadet training, physical fitness, and commitment.

What it meant to me. I do not see it as a finish line. It is evidence that the daily standard — training, preparation, fitness, and how I carried responsibility — was visible to other people.

Why it matters. It is one of the strongest formal markers in my cadet record, but the work behind it matters more than the medal itself.
