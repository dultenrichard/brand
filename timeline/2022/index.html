2022 · Training

Cadet Activity Program 1

What it is. CAP 1, completed on August 26, 2022, was an early formal cadet training milestone.

What it meant to me. The value was not the certificate by itself. It was exposure to a structured training environment where standards, teamwork, and participation were expected every day.

Why it matters. It sits between joining cadets and the later instructor/staff roles. It is part of the progression.

2022 · Training

Math League recognition

What it is. Recognition through the Kingston Math League Fun and Excellence in Mathematics tournament on June 6, 2022.

What it meant to me. Math competitions reward a different kind of performance: working through unfamiliar problems without being able to bluff your way through them.

Why it matters. It is one of the earlier academic markers in a record that later included senior university-preparation courses and Honour Roll.

2022 · Training

3rd Place · Intermediate Poem

What it is. A third-place result in the Royal Canadian Legion Branch 387 Remembrance Day poem competition.

What it meant to me. This was the third consecutive year with a Legion poetry placing. The exact rank changed; the consistent part was returning to the work.

Why it matters. For me, consistency matters more than building a mythology around a single win.
