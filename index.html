About

I’m Dulten, a Grade 12 student in Brockville. I’m interested in political science, international affairs, public service, and entrepreneurship.

Since joining Air Cadets in 2021, I’ve spent time instructing, mentoring, and learning how to lead people. Outside cadets, I work at Value Village, play rugby, and put time into practical projects. I’m interested in what people do with an idea once it leaves the page.
