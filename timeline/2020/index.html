2nd Place · Junior Poem

A second-place result in the Royal Canadian Legion Branch 387 Remembrance Day poem competition, Junior category.

Writing for Remembrance Day forced me to take an idea, give it structure, and make it mean something to somebody else. That basic skill shows up later in speeches, advocacy, research, and leadership.

It is an early marker of communication — not because a poem makes someone a leader, but because learning to communicate clearly starts somewhere.
