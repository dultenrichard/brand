Honour Roll

Academic recognition for the 2023–2024 school year.

Honour Roll is straightforward: the work was strong enough, consistently enough, to be formally recognized.

It matters because academic credibility is part of the larger record I am building toward political science, public service, and future professional work.

2024 · Consistency

Bronze Attendance Award

Attendance recognition at 851 Prince Edward RCACS for the 2023–2024 training year.

Attendance awards are easy to dismiss until you lead people. Then you learn how valuable it is when someone can simply be counted on to be there.

Reliability is not the most exciting leadership trait, but without it the rest is mostly talk.

Commanding Officer Commendation

A squadron commendation for contribution at 851 Squadron.

A commendation for contribution is different from winning a competition. It reflects usefulness to the unit.

That is closer to how I want to be judged: did I make the team better, more capable, or easier to rely on?

Most Improved Marksman

Range Safety Officer recognition for improvement in marksmanship.

The word I care about here is improved. It records progress, not natural ability.

It is a reminder that development is measurable when you keep working at something.

Jim Lounsberry Memorial Award · Top Fundraiser

The 851 Squadron memorial award recognizing top fundraising performance.

Fundraising required repetition, public interaction, and the willingness to keep asking people to support the squadron.

It connects directly to later sales/customer-service work: approach people properly, explain the purpose, and represent the organization well.
