2023 · Momentum

Cadet Activity Program 2

CAP 2, completed during summer 2023, continued my formal cadet development after CAP 1.

It moved the progression forward. Each course mattered because it gave me more context for what good instruction and leadership looked like before I was expected to provide either.

This is part of the foundation behind later FSI and staff-cadet responsibilities.

2023 · Momentum

Best All Around Student

School recognition received on June 18, 2023.

I value this one because 'all around' is broader than being strongest in a single subject. It reflects the idea that how you contribute matters alongside what you score.

It fits the way I think about development now: academics, work, leadership, sport, and service should reinforce each other.

2023 · Momentum

E.T.F.O. Award

An award presented at Athol-South Marysburgh School in 2023. My record confirms the award; I am keeping the public description conservative until the original criteria are attached to the site.

I would rather understate an award than invent what it meant. The certificate belongs in the record, and the exact criteria should come from the source.

That approach — evidence first — is the standard I want across this site.

2023 · Momentum

Spring Tagging Awards

At 851 Squadron, I received 2nd Place Tagging and Top Overall Tagging recognition in spring 2023.

Tagging is not glamorous. It is public-facing fundraising work, done repeatedly, while representing the squadron properly.

The awards matter because they recognize follow-through in routine work, not just performance when people are watching.

2023 · Momentum

First paid employment

I started customer-facing work at Sunflower Fields and later Currah’s Park Store & Grill.

Paid work changes the standard. Being helpful is no longer optional; people are depending on you to show up, communicate, and do the job.

That experience became the base for later retail and customer-service work.
