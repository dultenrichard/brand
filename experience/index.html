I joined Air Cadets in 2021 and have served with 851 Squadron and 661 Lt W F Sharpe Squadron. My experience has grown from learning the fundamentals to taking responsibility as a Warrant Officer Second Class.

Early cadet training progression

I completed Cadet Activity Program 1 on August 26, 2022 and CAP 2 during summer 2023. Those programs form part of the foundation before my later instructor and staff-cadet roles.

Instruction, mentorship, and responsibility

At 661 Squadron, my work has included delivering instruction, mentoring junior cadets, and supporting squadron activities. Working with a smaller team of senior cadets has made preparation, clear communication, and personal example especially important.

These responsibilities have taught me to think beyond my own performance: a lesson needs to be understood, a task needs to be followed through, and younger cadets need someone they can approach.

Areas of experience: lesson delivery, mentoring, teamwork, organization, and leadership through example.

Staff Cadet Sergeant · 2026

I returned to Blackdown in 2026 as a Staff Cadet Sergeant supporting Fitness and Sports Instructor training. My assignments included two weeks as a platoon sergeant and four weeks in a company float and support role.

The platoon role gave me responsibility within a defined team. Company support required flexibility across instruction, administration, logistics, cadet welfare, and daily training operations. It meant helping where needed, coordinating with others, and responding to the practical demands of the training day.

What I took from it: leadership depends on consistent preparation and follow-through, including the routine work that keeps a team functioning.
