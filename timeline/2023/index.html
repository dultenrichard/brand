2023 · Momentum

Cadet Activity Program 2

What it is. CAP 2, completed during summer 2023, continued my formal cadet development after CAP 1.

What it meant to me. It moved the progression forward. Each course mattered because it gave me more context for what good instruction and leadership looked like before I was expected to provide either.

Why it matters. This is part of the foundation behind later FSI and staff-cadet responsibilities.

2023 · Momentum

Best All Around Student

What it is. School recognition received on June 18, 2023.

What it meant to me. I value this one because 'all around' is broader than being strongest in a single subject. It reflects the idea that how you contribute matters alongside what you score.

Why it matters. It fits the way I think about development now: academics, work, leadership, sport, and service should reinforce each other.

2023 · Momentum

E.T.F.O. Award

What it is. An award presented at Athol-South Marysburgh School in 2023. My record confirms the award; I am keeping the public description conservative until the original criteria are attached to the site.

What it meant to me. I would rather understate an award than invent what it meant. The certificate belongs in the record, and the exact criteria should come from the source.

Why it matters. That approach — evidence first — is the standard I want across this site.

2023 · Momentum

Spring Tagging Awards

What it is. At 851 Squadron, I received 2nd Place Tagging and Top Overall Tagging recognition in spring 2023.

What it meant to me. Tagging is not glamorous. It is public-facing fundraising work, done repeatedly, while representing the squadron properly.

Why it matters. The awards matter because they recognize follow-through in routine work, not just performance when people are watching.

2023 · Momentum

First paid employment

What it is. I started customer-facing work at Sunflower Fields and later Currah’s Park Store & Grill.

What it meant to me. Paid work changes the standard. Being helpful is no longer optional; people are depending on you to show up, communicate, and do the job.

Why it matters. That experience became the base for later retail and customer-service work.
