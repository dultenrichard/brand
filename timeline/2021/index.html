2021 · A new direction

Joined Royal Canadian Air Cadets

The beginning of my cadet experience, initially with 851 Prince Edward RCACS.

At the start I was learning the system from the bottom: drill, dress, expectations, teamwork, and how a squadron functions. I did not join as a leader. I had to grow into responsibility.

The later awards make more sense when this starting point is visible. The progression matters more than pretending the senior roles appeared overnight.

2021 · A new direction

2nd Place · Intermediate Poem

A second consecutive second-place result in the Royal Canadian Legion Branch 387 Remembrance Day poem competition, this time in the Intermediate category.

Repeating a result matters differently than earning it once. It showed me that communication could be developed rather than treated as a one-off strength.

It continued a writing record that later became useful in mock trial, public speaking, research, and professional communication.
