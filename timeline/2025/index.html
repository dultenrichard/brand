2025 · Performance

Fitness & Sports Instructor

Completed the Royal Canadian Air Cadets Fitness and Sports Instructor qualification at Blackdown CTC on August 17, 2025.

The course combined physical performance with instruction. Knowing how to do something is different from being able to teach it clearly to somebody else.

It became the bridge between being a course cadet and returning to Blackdown as staff in 2026.

2025 · Performance

COSSA Rugby Silver Medalist

A silver-medal team result in the 2025 school rugby season.

Rugby is one of the best reminders I have that individual effort only matters if it serves the team. You can have a good game and still fail your job if the structure breaks around you.

The medal is a team result. That is why I value it.

2025 · Performance

Silver Attendance Award

Continued attendance recognition at 851 Squadron for the 2024–2025 training year.

A second attendance award is less about the colour changing from bronze to silver and more about sustained presence over another year.

Consistency compounds. That principle applies well beyond cadets.

2025 · Performance

theROC Bright Future Award nomination

A May 2025 nomination for the theROC Bright Future Award. This is a nomination, not an award win.

I keep nominations clearly separated from wins. Being nominated still means somebody believed the work was worth putting forward.

The distinction matters because this site is supposed to be a record, not an advertisement that stretches the truth.

April 2025 · PECI · Grade 10

Two Best Advocate awards

Two Best Advocate recognitions earned through mock trial in April 2025.

Advocacy is not volume. It is understanding the facts, deciding what matters, building a case, and explaining it in a way another person can follow.

Those awards connect directly to my interest in political science, law, research, and public service.

April 2025 · PECI · Grade 10

Best Closing

A Best Closing recognition earned through mock trial in April 2025.

A closing forces you to reduce a large amount of information into the argument that actually decides the question.

That skill matters everywhere I want to go: briefings, research, leadership, policy, and professional communication.
