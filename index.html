About

I’m Dulten, a Grade 12 student in Ontario. I’m interested in political science and international affairs.

A lot of my time goes into Air Cadets, work, and sport. I’ve instructed fitness and sports, worked in retail and hospitality, and competed in rugby and rowing. These are different parts of my life; this site is a place to keep them together.

I’m also working on Equinox, an Edel 665 sailboat, and developing an idea for practical leadership workshops for youth teams.

“To be a leader is to be liquid.”

— A thought I keep coming back to.
