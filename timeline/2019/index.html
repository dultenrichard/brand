Prince Edward County 4-H

What it is. Participation in the Maple Syrup Club and Pizza Club is part of my earliest documented community and practical-learning record.

What it meant to me. It was simple, hands-on learning. There was no résumé strategy behind it. That is exactly why it matters to me now: it was experience before I was thinking in terms of experience.

Why it matters. It belongs in the timeline because it shows the starting point — curiosity, participation, and learning through doing.
