2024 · Consistency

Honour Roll

What it is. Academic recognition for the 2023–2024 school year.

What it meant to me. Honour Roll is straightforward: the work was strong enough, consistently enough, to be formally recognized.

Why it matters. It matters because academic credibility is part of the larger record I am building toward political science, public service, and future professional work.

2024 · Consistency

Bronze Attendance Award

What it is. Attendance recognition at 851 Prince Edward RCACS for the 2023–2024 training year.

What it meant to me. Attendance awards are easy to dismiss until you lead people. Then you learn how valuable it is when someone can simply be counted on to be there.

Why it matters. Reliability is not the most exciting leadership trait, but without it the rest is mostly talk.

2024 · Consistency

Commanding Officer Commendation

What it is. A squadron commendation for contribution at 851 Squadron.

What it meant to me. A commendation for contribution is different from winning a competition. It reflects usefulness to the unit.

Why it matters. That is closer to how I want to be judged: did I make the team better, more capable, or easier to rely on?

Most Improved Marksman

What it is. Range Safety Officer recognition for improvement in marksmanship.

What it meant to me. The word I care about here is improved. It records progress, not natural ability.

Why it matters. It is a reminder that development is measurable when you keep working at something.

Jim Lounsberry Memorial Award · Top Fundraiser

What it is. The 851 Squadron memorial award recognizing top fundraising performance.

What it meant to me. Fundraising required repetition, public interaction, and the willingness to keep asking people to support the squadron.

Why it matters. It connects directly to later sales/customer-service work: approach people properly, explain the purpose, and represent the organization well.
