What this site does

This is my personal website. It presents my experience and provides links to contact me. It has no visitor accounts, advertising profiles, or on-site message database.

Optional analytics

Umami Cloud analytics are off until you enable them in this browser. The account uses the EU region. The site honours Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. Analytics are disabled on the private review domain.

When enabled on the public site, analytics may measure page views and selected interactions, such as opening navigation, applying a category filter, or downloading a document. Search text, contact messages, names, and email addresses are not sent as event data. URL query strings are excluded.

Analytics & motion controls

Analytics are optional and off by default.

These buttons store your choice locally in this browser. The footer’s motion control pauses decorative movement. Reduced-motion preferences from your device are also respected.

Contact & search

The contact composer prepares a mailto draft in your email app. Nothing typed into it is submitted to this website. Search runs locally against this site’s public records. If you send an email, your message is handled by the email services involved.

Hosting

The source is maintained on GitHub. This private review is hosted through ChatGPT Sites; the intended public host is GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical request information under their own policies.
