2025 · Performance

Fitness & Sports Instructor

What it is. Completed the Royal Canadian Air Cadets Fitness and Sports Instructor qualification at Blackdown CTC on August 17, 2025.

What it meant to me. The course combined physical performance with instruction. Knowing how to do something is different from being able to teach it clearly to somebody else.

Why it matters. It became the bridge between being a course cadet and returning to Blackdown as staff in 2026.

2025 · Performance

COSSA Rugby Silver Medalist

What it is. A silver-medal team result in the 2025 school rugby season.

What it meant to me. Rugby is one of the best reminders I have that individual effort only matters if it serves the team. You can have a good game and still fail your job if the structure breaks around you.

Why it matters. The medal is a team result. That is why I value it.

2025 · Performance

Silver Attendance Award

What it is. Continued attendance recognition at 851 Squadron for the 2024–2025 training year.

What it meant to me. A second attendance award is less about the colour changing from bronze to silver and more about sustained presence over another year.

Why it matters. Consistency compounds. That principle applies well beyond cadets.

2025 · Performance

theROC Bright Future Award nomination

What it is. A May 2025 nomination for the theROC Bright Future Award. This is a nomination, not an award win.

What it meant to me. I keep nominations clearly separated from wins. Being nominated still means somebody believed the work was worth putting forward.

Why it matters. The distinction matters because this site is supposed to be a record, not an advertisement that stretches the truth.
